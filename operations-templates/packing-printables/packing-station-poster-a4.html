1 Confirm & pick

  • Payment verified (prepaid / COD noted)
  • Match order ID to SKU & size
  • Inspect item — no defect / stain

2 Wrap in tissue

  • Fold garment neatly (no creases on display side)
  • Wrap fully in tissue paper
  • Place thank-you card on top

3 Seal polybag

  • Put wrapped item + invoice inside polybag
  • Peel & seal — no gaps at opening
  • Add branded sticker seal if available

4 Label & handover

  • Print courier label from admin / Shiprocket
  • Stick label flat on front — barcode scannable
  • Mark shipped + send tracking to customer
